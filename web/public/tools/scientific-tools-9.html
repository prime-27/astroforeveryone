Vedanga Time Units

  • 1 Yuga = 5 Solar Years
  • 60 Solar Months = 62 Synodic Lunar Months
  • Tithi = Lunar day (1/30th of synodic month)
  • Nakshatras = 27 stellar segments (13° 20' each)

Section 3: Mean vs. True Motion (Manda & Shighra)

Siddhantic astronomers (Aryabhata, Brahmagupta, Bhaskara) applied epicyclic corrections (Mandaphala and Shighraphala) to convert mean planetary positions into true observable positions.

Katapayadi Numerical Cipher System

An ancient Indian system of numerical representation used in astronomical treatises to encode constants (like Sine tables and π) into memorable verses or poetry.

Rule Table

• Ka-varga (क ट प य): 1 to 5
• Ta-varga (ट): 1 to 5
• Pa-varga (प): 1 to 5
• Ya-varga (य र ल व श ष स ह): 1 to 8
*Note: Read consonants right-to-left to form numbers. Vowels attached to consonants are ignored.
